## FAQ

### 1. What does this tool do?
It analyze your AI tool subscription and identifies opportunities to reduce costs by switching plans or alternatives.

### 2. Do I need to create an account?
No. you can run the audit instantly without signing up.

### 3. How accurate are the recommendations?
Recommendations are based on current pricing data and common patterns. They provide strong guidance, not financial advice.

### 4. Will my data be stored?
Only if you choose to save your report via email. Otherwise, no personal data is stored.

### 5. What happens if I have high savings?
If your potential saving are significant, you'll have the option to connect with Credex for deeper optimization.
